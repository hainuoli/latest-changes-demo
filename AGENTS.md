# Repository instructions

- Before every agent-created commit, show the changes, validation results, and
  exact title. Obtain explicit approval for that commit only.
- Use a single Gitmoji title with a capitalized English imperative subject,
  at most 72 characters, no trailing period, no body, and no trailers.
- Automated release-note commits are part of the approved acceptance test;
  they must also use a single Gitmoji title with no body.
